Lounge Planner

Works in any browser · best on laptop/desktop

A drag-and-drop floor planner for the department lounge. Arrange furniture to scale, see how many people fit, and share layouts with the group. This card is ordered most-important first — the last section is purely nice-to-have.

Start here

Three steps to your first layout

  1. Add furniture. Click any item in the left panel (sofas, tables, chairs, and so on). It drops into the middle of the room.
  2. Place it. Drag the piece where you want it. Drop in as many as you like.
  3. Adjust it. Click a piece to select it, then use the panel on the right to rename, resize, rotate, set how many it seats, or delete it.
Most important thing to know

The tool does not autosave, and closing the tab clears your work. Treat Export as “Save”: before you close, export your layout (see Save & share below). One quick export and it’s safe.

Everyday editing

Working with a piece

  • Move — drag it.
  • Resize — drag any corner handle.
  • Rotate — drag the round handle above it, or use Rotate 90°.
  • Rename / seats / size — type in the right-hand panel.
  • Duplicate or Delete — buttons in the right-hand panel.
  • Lock — stops a piece from moving. The mailboxes are locked by default; select and untick Locked to move them.
  • Snap to grid — toggle at top-left of the room. Each grid square is 1 ft.

Save & share

Getting layouts to each other

Use the Export menu (top-right). There are two kinds of output:

  • Pictures — PNG or SVG. Best for dropping into an email or a meeting agenda. Each picture includes a title block, a scale bar, and a legend, so it explains itself to anyone who has never opened the tool.
  • Editable copies — a share code (copies to your clipboard) or a .json file. Send either to a colleague; they open Load… and paste the code or pick the file to keep editing it.

Every file names itself automatically as title-author-timestamp, so fill in the Title and Author boxes at the top before exporting. Suggested workflow for the group: keep everyone’s .json files in one shared Drive folder — the names will sort and stay tidy on their own.

Reading the room

What the drawing is telling you

  • The room itself is fixed — walls, windows, the two doors and their swings, the hallway openings, the air vent, and the mailboxes are all built in and drawn to scale.
  • Red outline = a conflict. The piece overlaps another, pokes into a wall, or blocks a door, hallway, or the vent. Overlaps are allowed — the tool just flags them so nothing hides by accident.
  • Blue dashed zones mark clearance at doors and hallways; amber marks the vent keep-clear. Toggle them with Clearances.
  • Not sure what a symbol means? Open Legend (in the toolbar, or the panel on the right) for a full key and the scale.

How many fit

The capacity estimate

The panel at the top-right updates live as you work:

  • Seated counts the seats on the seating you’ve placed. Standing estimates how many can mingle in the leftover open floor. Total adds the two.
  • Set the standing comfort — Tight / Standard / Relaxed — to match the event, from a packed post-talk reception to a casual afternoon.
  • This is a comfort estimate for planning, not a fire-code occupancy limit.

Nice to have

Everything else, once you’re comfortable

  • Several layouts at once — use New and the Layout menu to keep alternatives side by side, then Compare… to view two or three together with their seat and area counts.
  • Notes — click ＋ Note, then click the plan to drop a label (“coffee station here”). Double-click a note to edit or remove it.
  • Tidy the view — Grid, Clearances, Legend, and Tally each toggle on and off, so you can strip the canvas down to just the furniture when you want to focus.
  • Custom pieces — Custom block… at the bottom of the catalog lets you type exact dimensions for anything not in the list.
